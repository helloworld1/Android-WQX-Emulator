WQX Emulator brings the classic GoldenGlobal WQX (文曲星) NC1020 electronic dictionary and handheld organizer to Android with fast, accurate native emulation.

Features:
- Authentic 6502 CPU, I/O, LCD, real-time clock, and DSP audio emulation written in native C
- Low-latency audio via AAudio / OpenSL ES for key beeps, melodies, English word pronunciation (发音), and Mandarin voice time announcement (报时)
- Automatic date and time synchronization with your Android device's system clock
- Built-in NC1020 dictionaries, organizer, schedule, calculator, GVBASIC, and classic games
- Portrait and landscape layouts with a realistic NC1020 keypad skin and release-triggered keys
- Turbo (SpeedUp) mode for fast-forwarding emulation
- Automatic and manual save states, plus factory reset support
- Material 3 interface with Light, Dark, and Follow System themes

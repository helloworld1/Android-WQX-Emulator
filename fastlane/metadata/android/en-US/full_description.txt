Relive the classic WQX NC1020 (文曲星) electronic dictionary and pocket computer on Android! From looking up words with retro voice speech to playing legendary RPGs and writing BASIC code, WQX Emulator brings back the authentic handheld experience.

Highlights:
• Built-in Classics: E-C/C-E dictionaries, calendar, schedule, calculator, GVBASIC, and nostalgic games including Legend of Heroes (英雄坛说), Exorcist (伏魔记), Block World, and Sokoban.
• Authentic Look & Sound: Classic green dot-matrix LCD with side status icons and pixel grid, plus crisp key beeps, melodies, English pronunciation (发音), and Mandarin time announcement (报时).
• Realistic Skins & Tactile Keypad: Choose between Original clamshell and Full LCD skins in portrait or landscape, with crisp vector keys, release-triggered taps, long-press hold, and haptic feedback.
• Built-in NOR File Manager: Browse, preview, import, and export WQX ebooks (.txt), GVBASIC programs (.bas), boot graphics (.ski), apps (.bin), and full .nor backups right on your phone.
• Modern Convenience: Auto clock sync, SpeedUp turbo mode, automatic & manual save states, and Material 3 Light/Dark themes.
